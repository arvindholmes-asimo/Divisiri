Mr. C.M.Reddy (Founder and CSO)

Amitojas is founded by an eminent scientist Mr. C.M.Reddy, a biochemist and medical nutritionist by training and a well seasoned professional with more than 18 years of experience in Nutraceuticals R&D. During his career as scientist in Research and Development domain, he was able to lead and mentor Product Development Teams for achieving the higher goals of the Organizations paving their ways to expand in Nutraceutical Markets worldwide.

Mr. Reddy has travelled extensively in Europe, US and Asia ( for nutraceutical R&D and technology transfer activities) and other parts of the world and participated in various conferences/ seminars to present his research outcomes. He has Participated in Various Food, Nutra and Pharma conferences conducted In India and Abroad, such as Nutraceuticals India, FI-Hi India, Food Regulation & Safety Summit, NUTRA SUMMIT, AYUTECH, CPHI, VITA FOODS, SUPPLYSIDE WEST, IFT, Fi Asia, Hi Japan, etc.

He is an expert in analyzing the Techno- commercial Feasibility of new projects in nutrition and wellness category, including technical due diligence of Nutrition Brands / Technology for Mergers and Acquisitions. He has several patents to his credit in nutritional ingredient and formulation sciences and currently working on many more out-of the box inventions in wellness field for the benefit of the mankind at large.

Mr. Reddy is a Visiting faculty to various colleges in Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Mumbai Delhi, Kolkata and other cities in India, enlightening Students on Future prospects of Nutrition and wellness sciences (including ayurceuticals) and also undertakes corporate training on advances in nutrion and wellness industry such as challenges in new Product Development, protection of Intellectual property rights and Regulatory Guidelines governing Nutraceuticals Such as FSSAI, EFSA, FDA, etc.

Mr. Venkata Siva Kalyan. D (COO)

Mr. Venkata Siva Kalyan is a young and dynamic entrant in to the Wellness Industry and he strongly believes that " True partnership between R&D Teams, Supply chain and Brand Promoters" is the key to successful new product launch in wellness industry. He holds a Bachelors Degree in Life Sciences and an MBA in marketing. Initially, he worked for a couple of years in a Multi National Bank in the Capacity of Customer Relationship Manager. Realising his passion in life sciences, he switched back to Pharma & Nutra sectors and worked for almost a decade in different organizations to gain hands on experience. With the perfect blend of experience in the domains of life sciences and marketing , he has mastered the art of Operations Management, Business Networking and Customer Relationship Management.

Mr. Kalyan is also a Pioneer in Institutional sales, Private labels, Public Relations including CSR activities, etc... He is specializes in marketing innovative products and dealing with technology transfers deals. He has travelled extensively to China, Hong Kong, UAE and Europe on business trips and has developed rich business contacts in overseas.

Mr. Kalyan along with Mr. C.M.Reddy, Founder of Amitojas, are tirelessly working on transforming Amitojas, from a pure R & D Company into a market leader in Nutrition and Wellness, expanding their business operations in areas such as IP creation and Tech Transfer, Private Labels to MNCs in India and abroad, Exports etc.. , while building strategic and longterm deals with the best of global R&D companies so as to provide the world class Nutrition and wellness products into the market, backed by Authentic scientific Data.
